Hawaii 1778 - credits

Author: cryptoboy2283

Relief, coastline and bathymetry: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model
(public domain), exported through the NCEI image service by tools/fetch_data.py.
Map format: Civ5MapImage / Civilization V WorldBuilder format description.
Leader portraits, diplomacy scenes, Dawn of Man images, emblems and the icons of the unique units and buildings were generated with
Black Forest Labs' FLUX.2-dev through the Together AI image API (art/generate_art.py) and assembled with ImageMagick (art/build_art.py).
The leaders are imagined; no real person is depicted. Unit and building art is the stock Firaxis art of the donor civilizations.
